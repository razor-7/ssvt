# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 14 March 2026**

SSVT Logistics ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who visit our website and interact with our services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

## Information We Collect

**Contact form submissions**: When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and the message you provide. We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry.

**Analytics data**: If you consent to analytics cookies, we collect anonymised data about how you use our website, including pages visited, time on site, and device type. This data is processed by Google Analytics and helps us improve our website experience.

**Technical data**: Our web server automatically records standard technical information including IP addresses, browser type, and pages accessed. This data is used for security monitoring and is not linked to personal identities.

## How We Use Your Information

- To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form
- To improve our website based on anonymised analytics
- To ensure the security and proper functioning of our website

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Retention

Contact form data is retained for 12 months from the date of submission and then securely deleted. Analytics data is retained for 26 months in accordance with Google Analytics retention settings.

## Cookies

We use a single analytics cookie (only with your consent) to understand how visitors use our site. You can accept or decline this cookie using the banner shown on your first visit. Your preference is stored in your browser's local storage. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser data.

## Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
